Goodreads

Why this is manual

Goodreads removed its public API in 2020 and its public shelf pages in 2026, and /user/sign_in is now a stub that hands off to Amazon's sign-in flow. There is no form to POST a password to, so a real browser session is the only way in — every maintained Goodreads tool works this way.

  1. Press Start browser — Chromium opens in the panel below.
  2. Sign in fully, including any OTP or CAPTCHA, until you can see your shelves.
  3. Press Save session.

The browser is reached through an authenticated tunnel on this page — its screen is never exposed on a port of its own. When the session expires, the dashboard says so and you come back here.
